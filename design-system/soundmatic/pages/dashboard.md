# Dashboard override

The approved SoundMatic mockup overrides the luxury/editorial master direction for operational screens.

- Layout: fixed midnight-navy sidebar on desktop; compact sticky top bar and drawer on mobile.
- Surfaces: warm-white content canvas, white cards, crisp slate borders, restrained elevation.
- Primary: indigo `#4F46E5`; success `#087F5B`; warning `#B45309`; danger `#B42318`.
- Typography: Inter/system sans throughout; dense dashboard rhythm with clear numeric hierarchy.
- Icons: one outline SVG family, 1.8–2px strokes; no emoji as structural controls.
- Interaction: 44px primary targets, visible focus, semantic labels, reduced-motion fallback.
- Data: status must always include text or icon in addition to color.
